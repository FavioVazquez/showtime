# Spring plant swap: details

- Name: Hollis Street Plant Swap
- Who runs it: the Hollis Street Community Garden volunteers
- Date: Saturday, April 18
- Time: 10 am to 1 pm
- Place: Hollis Street Community Garden, by the tool shed (rain or shine)
- How it works: bring a plant, cutting or seed packet, take one home. No money changes hands.
- Please label what you bring with its name and whether it likes sun or shade.
- Free, all ages, no sign-up needed.
