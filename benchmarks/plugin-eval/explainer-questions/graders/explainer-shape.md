The user asked for a 60-second explainer that stops and asks the viewer questions, and wanted the plan first.
PASS if the plan has all three of: a cold open that states the stakes or the puzzle in the first seconds (not a
title card or "In this video..."), a roadmap of the steps that the viewer is shown, and a close that ties back to
and answers the opening; and every question is asked before its answer is told, with choices to pick from.
FAIL if any of the cold open, the roadmap or the tie-back is missing, if a question comes after its answer was
already given, or if there is no plan at all.
