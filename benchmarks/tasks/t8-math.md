**What good looks like:** the L-shaped (gnomon) square-growing picture or an equally clear visual proof,
correct notation (1 + 3 + 5 + ... + (2n-1) = n²), each step on screen long enough to read, no algebra
errors. The fact-check judge checks the mathematics instead of a source file.
